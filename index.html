Apple Health

Direct Apple Health synchronisation is not available in this standalone web app. You can import exercise records from a CSV exported or prepared from Apple Health data using the Settings tab. Imported records are marked to help avoid duplicates.

Symptom trends

Daily averages for the last 30 days. Higher general/digestive scores mean worse symptoms; higher energy means better energy.

Exploratory associations

Compares same-day symptoms on days with versus without food categories or recorded exercise. These are observations, not evidence of causes. Delayed effects, missing entries and other factors can change the results.

Check-in reminders

Suggested times: 8:00 am, 1:00 pm and 8:00 pm. This offline web app cannot reliably send scheduled iPhone push notifications when closed. Use iPhone Reminders or Clock alarms for these times.

Apple Health CSV import

Import a CSV with columns date,activity,duration,intensity. Dates should be ISO timestamps or YYYY-MM-DD. An Apple Health export must first be converted into this format; raw Apple Health ZIP/XML is not supported.

Your data

Stored locally in this browser on this device. No account or server. Clearing browser data or using a different device may remove or hide records, so export backups regularly.
